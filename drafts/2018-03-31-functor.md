前陣子經 Jemery Lu 大大介紹了[好書](http://haskellbook.com/)，(又)開始不務正業唸起了 Haskell。看到一段程式碼深切感受到 Haskell 的美感。因此想試著來解釋一下它有趣的地方。

### 原程式碼：

```haskell
replaceWithP :: a -> Char
replaceWithP = const 'p'

lms :: [Maybe [Char]]
lms = [Just "Ave", Nothing, Just "woohoo"]

-- replace all
replaceWithP lms -- => 'p'

-- lift once
fmap replaceWithP lms -- => "ppp", or say ['p', 'p', 'p']

-- lift twice
(fmap . fmap) replaceWithP lms -- => [Just 'p', Nothing, Just 'p']

-- lift thrice
(fmap . fmap . fmap) replaceWithP lms -- => [Just "ppp", Nothing, Just "pppppp"]
```
